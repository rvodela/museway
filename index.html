Privacy first

Your data stays yours.

1

On-device storage. Triage history lives in local, encrypted storage — not a third-party server.

2

Read-only access. MuseWay never sends messages, emails, or files on your behalf — it only reads to summarize.

3

You control every connection. Disconnect Gmail, Drive, or WhatsApp any time from Settings — no lingering access.

4

Mood Mate photos are never saved. Analyzed in the moment, then gone.

🛡️
Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) is required before every triage run — MuseWay never runs silently in the background without your explicit go-ahead.
